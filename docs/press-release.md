## Kairos Launches Relationship Wellness App That Gives Couples a Daily Practice for Staying Connected

*A 30-second daily check-in, frictionless date planning, and a shared memory timeline -- designed for couples first, with a vision to serve any relationship that matters*

**San Francisco, CA -- 2026**

Today, Kairos announced the launch of its relationship wellness app for couples who want to be more intentional about their connection. Kairos provides a private, shared Space where two partners can check in on how they feel, plan meaningful time together, and build a living archive of their shared experiences -- all synced in real time across devices.

Unlike social or messaging apps, Kairos focuses on *reflection*, not communication. The core product is a 30-second daily practice that, over weeks and months, makes the invisible trajectory of a relationship visible.

### The Problem

Couples don't break up because of one catastrophic event. They drift apart slowly, in the space between conversations that never happen and dates that keep getting postponed. By the time things feel off, they're often months into a pattern neither person noticed forming.

There are plenty of tools for communicating -- texting, calling, social media. But there is almost nothing for *reflecting* on how a relationship actually feels day to day. Kairos closes that gap.

### The Solution

**Useful from the first minute -- even before your partner joins.**

Most couple apps are useless until both partners sign up. Kairos is different. A single user can check in, see their personal trend, plan moments, and start building their reflection habit immediately. When their partner joins, the experience deepens -- check-ins combine into a shared Relationship Health Score, the dashboard updates in real time, and a shared memory timeline begins.

**Pulse Check-ins** -- Each partner picks 3 of 5 relationship attributes (Connection, Intimacy, Peace, Trust, Expression) that matter most to them. Every day, they rate each on a 1-100 scale. Shared picks are weighted double. The result is a Relationship Health Score (0-100) visualized as an animated Voronoi mosaic that shifts from blue to red as the score rises. Six insight labels -- Thriving, Growing, Steady, Cooling, Struggling, Just starting -- give the couple a word for what the numbers mean.

**Streaks that matter.** Kairos tracks your check-in streak with a visible flame icon on the dashboard. Miss a day and the flame dims. A configurable daily reminder ("Time to check in -- your 14-day streak is at risk") keeps the habit alive without being annoying. A weekly digest notification summarizes how your score moved, creating a natural reason to return even on weeks when you skip a day.

**Planned Moments** -- Couples schedule three types of intentional time: Connect (a date with a time-of-day slot), Celebrate (an occasion), and Escape (a multi-day trip). A progressive-reveal planner with preset suggestions, inline calendars, and a slide-to-save gesture makes scheduling a 30-second task. Moments sync to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.

**Swipe to Reflect** -- When a planned moment passes, each partner independently reflects. Swipe right: "Lived it." Swipe left: "Missed it." Both create a memory -- because even a cancelled date is worth acknowledging. The swipe card teaches itself: first-time users see the card gently tilt right and left, revealing what each direction does, until they touch it.

**Shared Memories** -- After a moment, each partner creates their own memory: photos, a caption, a place, a song, and an optional embedded pulse check-in. The Memories tab displays a unified timeline with synced photo and reflection sliders. Tapping any photo opens a fullscreen gallery with pinch-to-zoom. Emoji reactions add a lightweight social layer.

**Guided First Experience** -- On first launch, before the dashboard even loads, Kairos walks you through your first check-in. In 30 seconds, you adjust three sliders, see the Voronoi mosaic come alive with your colors, and understand the core value: "This is how your relationship feels right now. Check in tomorrow to see how it changes." That single moment of delight is designed to hook you before you even invite your partner.

### What Makes Kairos Different

**It works alone.** Most couple apps are useless until both partners join. Kairos shows your personal check-in trend, tracks your streak, and lets you plan moments from day one. Your partner joining makes it better, but isn't required for value.

**It's a practice, not a tool.** The daily check-in takes 30 seconds but creates a compounding awareness over weeks and months. The streak system turns it into a habit.

**The health score is honest.** Per-check-in config snapshots mean historical scores are never distorted by changing priorities. The score reflects how things actually felt at the time.

**Planning is frictionless.** Progressive-reveal planner, quick date pills, stretchy time slider, slide-to-save. Every excuse not to plan has been removed.

**Real-time everything.** When your partner checks in, your dashboard updates live -- the mosaic re-animates, the activity trail refreshes, and a push notification lands.

### The Bigger Vision

Kairos launches for couples, but the core mechanics -- check-ins, planned moments, shared memories, health scores -- apply to any relationship that matters. Friend groups that drift after college. Siblings who stop calling. Families scattered across time zones. The plan is to validate the daily practice with couples (who have the strongest built-in motivation), then expand to any close-knit group that wants to stay intentionally connected.

4. Invite your partner when you're ready.
